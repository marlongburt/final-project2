History

Matcha Cafe Maiko originated in Waikiki, Hawaii, opening its first location in July 2016. The brand was inspired by traditional Japanese dessert cafes and expanded quickly across North America, growing to over 25 franchise locations in 15 U.S. states. Sourcing ceremonial-grade green tea directly from Harima-en (Harima Garden), a family-run farm in Uji, Kyoto operating since 1858. Modeled after long-standing Japanese green tea houses, focusing on items made from scratch like soft serve, parfaits, and lattes.

Matcha Cafe Maiko has authentic Japanese treats featuring ceremonial-grade matcha and hojicha sourced from Kyoto's historic Harima Garden, specializing in signature soft-serve ice cream, loaded parfaits, and handcrafted lattes. I usually get their soft serve ice cream, typically half-vanilla and half-matcha, but sometimes I get the half-yuzu and half-lychee flavor.
