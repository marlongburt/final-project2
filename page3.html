History

Powder, located at 260 Divisadero Street in San Francisco, originally launched as a specialty shop focused on Taiwanese-style shaved snow made from organic Straus dairy. Expanding its menu, the dessert bar later introduced traditional Korean bingsu featuring micro-shaved milk snow, sweet milk glaze, misutgaru (multi-grain powder), azuki red bean, and mochi. Powder in San Francisco was founded and opened in September 2016 by former tech workers David Chung and Mimi Hanley.

Powder is a desert shop serves Taiwanese-style shaved snow made with Straus organic dairy, it has rotating flavors like black sesame, Vietnamese coffee, and ube alongside customizable toppings. There are many different syrup flavors and toppings to choose from. This spot is my absolute favorite place to go in sf. My go-to order is the mango shaved ice with passion fruit syrup and fresh strawberries.
