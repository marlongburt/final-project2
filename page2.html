History

In-N-Out Burger was founded in 1948 by Harry and Esther Snyder in Baldwin Park, California, introducing the state's first drive-thru stand featuring a revolutionary two-way speaker system. Staying family-owned and stubbornly traditional, the regional chain grew slowly while maintaining a famously simple menu.

In-N-Out is a fast-food chain known for its fresh, never-frozen beef and unique menu items. It specializes in simple, high-quality burgers, milkshakes, and fries. My favorite thing to order is a cheeseburger and their pink lemonade.
